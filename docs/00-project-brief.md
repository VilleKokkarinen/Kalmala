# Project brief

## One-line pitch

In a mythic northern wilderness where every substance has a spirit and a physical consequence, up to four wanderers build a sanctuary, master runic craft, and descend into shifting underworlds to restore a broken world-song.

## Player promise

Players should feel clever when they combine materials, magic, terrain, shelter, weather, and teamwork to solve a dangerous problem. A prepared camp should feel safe but never permanent; the wilderness and its supernatural forces should invite expeditions.

## Product frame

- **Genre:** third-person cooperative survival-action RPG with procedural expedition spaces.
- **Platform target:** Windows PC first.
- **Engine:** Unreal Engine 5, C++ foundations with Blueprint-facing gameplay APIs.
- **Session model:** solo, listen server, and dedicated server; 1–4 players for the first release.
- **Camera:** close third person; optional first-person evaluation after the vertical slice.
- **Business model:** undecided. Do not implement monetization systems.

## Creative pillars

1. **Living matter:** heat, water, wind, wood, stone, metal, ash, and magical residue have comprehensible interactions.
2. **Shelter against the wild:** construction, warmth, food, and wards turn a hostile land into home.
3. **Songs make magic:** magic is crafted from discovered verses, materials, and ritual context—not a generic spell list.
4. **Kalevala-inflected original fantasy:** rune-singing, lake-country, ironworking, birch forests, carved wood, woven patterns, and long northern twilight guide the mood. Create original characters, locations, symbols, and narrative.
5. **Cooperation with consequences:** distinct player choices combine productively; friendly fire and environmental risks are intentional but configurable.

## Scope guardrails

The first playable release is a **vertical slice**, not an open-world simulation. It needs one biome, one camp location, three enemy archetypes, a small handcrafted expedition, basic building, one magical discipline, and a complete co-op session loop.

## Reference boundary

The project may learn from systemic simulation, emergent spell interactions, survival progression, and co-op building. It must not reproduce specific game content, UI, terminology, characters, story, assets, maps, enemy designs, or code from any reference work.
